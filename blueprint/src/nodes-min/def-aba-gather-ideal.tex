\begin{definition}[The gather implementation over broadcast specifications]
  \label{def:aba-gather-ideal}
  \lean{PLTS.ABA.Gather.GaMsg}\lean{PLTS.ABA.Gather.PRec}\lean{PLTS.ABA.Gather.IdealState}\lean{PLTS.ABA.Gather.IdealStep}\lean{PLTS.ABA.Gather.idealInst}\lean{PLTS.ABA.Gather.idealInst_step_iff_row}\leanok
  \uses{def:aba-gather-sub, def:aba-gather-spec, def:aba-brb-spec, def:aba-networkstate}
  The gather instance of Definition~\ref{def:aba-gather-sub} whose broadcast tier is $2n$ broadcast specifications: \leandecl{PLTS.ABA.Gather.idealInst}.
  Its rows are Algorithm~4 of~\cite{LeslieBP}, the binding form of Algorithm~5 of~\cite{AFW25}, stated over the composition's state: each process contributes its input and its $\mathrm{BIND}$ payload through its own broadcast instance (D32), an entry set is approved at a process when that process's input store holds every entry of it, and the $\mathrm{ECHO}$/$\mathrm{VOTE}$ exchange over approved sets runs on the gather network.
  A return requires $n{-}f$ bind stores below the output and the output below the input stores, and the gather network writes the core at the first of them (D29): \leandecl{PLTS.ABA.Gather.IdealStep}, matched against the transitions of the composition by \leandecl{PLTS.ABA.Gather.idealInst_step_iff_row}.
\end{definition}
