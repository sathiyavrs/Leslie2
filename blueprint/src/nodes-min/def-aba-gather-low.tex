\begin{definition}[The gather implementation over Bracha instances]
  \label{def:aba-gather-low}
  \lean{PLTS.ABA.Gather.StateOverBracha}\lean{PLTS.ABA.Gather.StepOverBracha}\lean{PLTS.ABA.Gather.instanceOverBracha}\lean{PLTS.ABA.Gather.instanceOverBracha_step_iff_row}\leanok
  \uses{def:aba-gather-sub, def:aba-gather-ideal, def:aba-brb-impl}
  The gather instance of Definition~\ref{def:aba-gather-sub} whose broadcast tier is $2n$ Bracha instances of Definition~\ref{def:aba-brb-impl}: \leandecl{PLTS.ABA.Gather.instanceOverBracha}.
  Its rows are those of Definition~\ref{def:aba-gather-ideal} with each broadcast row carrying a Bracha row in its place, and the internal rows of the $2n$ instances --- their deliveries, echoes, votes and injections --- are the silent rows of the composition: \leandecl{PLTS.ABA.Gather.StepOverBracha}, matched against the transitions of the composition by \leandecl{PLTS.ABA.Gather.instanceOverBracha_step_iff_row}.
  The gather tier is the same at both tiers, so the stores, the guards that read them and the core are written here as they are one tier up (D29).
\end{definition}
