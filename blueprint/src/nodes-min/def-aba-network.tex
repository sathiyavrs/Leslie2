\begin{definition}[The network]
  \label{def:aba-network}
  \lean{PLTS.ABA.ABDY.NetworkState}\lean{PLTS.ABA.ABDY.NetworkState.corrupt}\lean{PLTS.ABA.ABDY.abdyGhostStep}\lean{PLTS.ABA.Implementation.NetworkStep.announceBound}\lean{PLTS.ABA.ABDY.NetworkStep}\lean{PLTS.ABA.ABDY.network}\leanok
  \uses{def:aba-programs, def:aba-labels, def:aba-parameters}
  The one local state holding what no process may see: the per-sender sent sets of the round messages and of the DECIDED payloads, the corrupted set with its budget, which guards its $\mathrm{fail}$ transition (D1), and the rounds a correct process has started, which with $k \in F$ guard its two outputs on a corrupted process's behalf and its round injection (D11, D37): \leandecl{PLTS.ABA.ABDY.network}.
  Its ghost for a round is one bit, the round's bound bit: the round's first return records \leandecl{PLTS.ABA.GBCA.ByABDY.boundOf} of the round's messages, a later return leaves it, and the network announces the bit it holds by \leandeclnamed{announceBound}{PLTS.ABA.Implementation.NetworkStep.announceBound}, its read of the ghost being the identity (D29, D30).
\end{definition}
