\begin{definition}[Auxiliary-variable removal]
  \label{def:auxiliary-variable-removal}
  \lean{PLTS.AuxiliaryVariableRemoval}\lean{PLTS.System.LabelSaturated}\lean{PLTS.System.IdleOn}\lean{PLTS.SeparatesSilent}\lean{PLTS.AuxiliaryVariableRemoval.achievableTraceDists_eq}\leanok
  \uses{def:forwardsim, def:achievable, def:strongfunctional, thm:forward-sound}
  An auxiliary-variable removal of $sys_A$ onto $sys_0$ is a map $\pi$ on states, an identification $\varphi$ of labels and a set $K$ of labels, under which a transition of $sys_A$ on a label of $K$ leaves the projection where it stands, every other transition of $sys_A$ projects along $\pi$ to a transition of $sys_0$ on the same label, every transition of $sys_0$ lifts to a transition of $sys_A$ up to $\varphi$, and $\tau$ is the one label with the $\varphi$-image of $\tau$: \leandecl{PLTS.AuxiliaryVariableRemoval}, \leandecl{PLTS.SeparatesSilent}.
  At $\varphi = \mathrm{id}$, with every label of $K$ silent, the two systems have the same achievable trace distributions; hiding takes a general $\varphi$ and $K$ there, and parallel composition against a component that is label-saturated for $\varphi$ and idle on $K$, abstraction and restriction preserve a removal: \leandecl{PLTS.AuxiliaryVariableRemoval.achievableTraceDists_eq}, \leandecl{PLTS.System.LabelSaturated}, \leandecl{PLTS.System.IdleOn}.
\end{definition}
