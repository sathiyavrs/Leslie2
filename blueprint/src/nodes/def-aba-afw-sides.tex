\begin{definition}[The gather-based sides and their protocol-shaped systems]
  \label{def:aba-afw-sides}
  \lean{PLTS.ABA.AFW.corruptionOverBracha}\lean{PLTS.ABA.AFW.corruptionOverBroadcastSpecification}\lean{PLTS.ABA.AFW.corruptionOverGatherSpecifications}\lean{PLTS.ABA.AFW.roundFamilyOverBracha}\lean{PLTS.ABA.AFW.roundFamilyOverBroadcastSpecification}\lean{PLTS.ABA.AFW.roundFamilyOverGatherSpecifications}\lean{PLTS.ABA.AFW.composed}\lean{PLTS.ABA.AFW.composedOverBroadcastSpecification}\lean{PLTS.ABA.AFW.composedOverGatherSpecifications}\leanok
  \uses{def:aba-gbca-gather-low, def:aba-gbcasub, def:aba-composed, def:idle-family, def:process-algebra, def:relabel}
  Each reading of the two-gather round speaks the extended alphabet $\mathrm{ExtendedLabel}(n)$ itself: a round-tagged label, a Byzantine handshake row included, takes the reading's own row, and every other extended label idles.
  Each reading is gathered into the $\mathbb{N}$-indexed family over the ownership map $\mathrm{roundOwnsLabel}$, with $\mathrm{fail}(k)$ the broadcast that corrupts every round's coordinates in lockstep (D1): the sides $\mathrm{roundFamilyOverBracha}$, $\mathrm{roundFamilyOverBroadcastSpecification}$ and $\mathrm{roundFamilyOverGatherSpecifications}$.
  Each side, beside the round loops, the ABA-side network and the coin oracle and through the two hiding frames of the composed reading's pipeline, gives a protocol-shaped system: $\afw{\mathrm{composed}}(P)$, $\afw{\mathrm{hybrid}}_1(P)$ and $\afw{\mathrm{hybrid}}_2(P)$. The first reads the gather-based protocol as a composition, and stands to the gather-based implementation exactly as $\abdy{\mathrm{composed}}(P)$ of Definition~\ref{def:aba-composed} stands to the direct one; the other two are the stages the substitution below passes through.
  Full construction: \leandecl{PLTS.ABA.AFW.composed}.
\end{definition}
