\begin{definition}[The composed system]
  \label{def:aba-composed}
  \lean{PLTS.ABA.Composition.RoundLoopStep}\lean{PLTS.ABA.Composition.roundLoopProgram}\lean{PLTS.ABA.Composition.ABANetworkState}\lean{PLTS.ABA.Composition.ABANetworkState.corrupt}\lean{PLTS.ABA.Composition.ABANetworkStep}\lean{PLTS.ABA.Composition.ABANetwork}\lean{PLTS.ABA.Composition.ComposedState}\lean{PLTS.ABA.ABDY.composedExtended}\lean{PLTS.ABA.ABDY.composedHidden}\lean{PLTS.ABA.ABDY.composed}\leanok
  \uses{def:aba-gbca-instance-by-abdy, def:aba-programs, def:aba-network, def:aba-protocol, def:aba-round-loop-state, def:relabel, def:synchronised-product, def:process-algebra}
  The protocol read as a composition of four components over the extended alphabet $\mathrm{ExtendedLabel}(n, M)$ at ABDY22's round messages: the family $\mathrm{gbcaInstanceFamily}(P)$ of round instances, the round loop $\mathrm{roundLoopProgram}(P,j)$ of each process, the ABA network $\mathrm{ABANetwork}(P)$ holding the DECIDED sets (D12$'$), the corrupted set with its budget and the rounds a correct process has started (D37), and the common coin joined through $\mathrm{coinLabelMap}$.
  \[
    \abdy{\mathrm{composedExtended}}(P) \;=\;
      \mathrm{gbcaInstanceFamily}(P) \parallel \bigl(\mathrm{synchronisedProduct}_{j}\ \mathrm{roundLoopProgram}(P,j)
        \parallel (\mathrm{ABANetwork}(P) \parallel \mathrm{coinOverRoundAlphabet}(P))\bigr)
  \]
  is composed component by component, as Definition~\ref{def:aba-protocol} states, giving $\abdy{\mathrm{composedHidden}}(P)$ under the inner hiding and $\abdy{\mathrm{composed}}(P)$ under the outer.
  A round loop is not round-filtered: it takes every round's $\mathrm{callG}$, its own as a participant and every other process's on an idle transition.
  Each label is the output of one component.
  A round loop outputs its graded-agreement call, its coin call, its DECIDED send and relay and its ABA return; the ABA network outputs the DECIDED deliveries, $\mathrm{fail}$ and a corrupted process's two calls, under $k \in F$ and at a round a correct process has started (D11, D37); the round family outputs the returns $\mathrm{retG}$ and the announcements $\mathrm{boundG}$; and the coin outputs $\mathrm{retW}$.
  Every component takes its inputs at every state, a round loop taking a return of a round or a coin it does not wait for on $\mathrm{retGLoop}$ or $\mathrm{retWLoop}$ and a DECIDED delivery of a payload it holds on $\mathrm{decidedDeliverLoop}$, and the outputs are pairwise disjoint (Definition~\ref{def:input-enabledness}): \leandeclnamed{roundLoop_inputEnabled}{PLTS.ABA.Composition.roundLoop_inputEnabled}, \leandeclnamed{abaNetwork_inputEnabled}{PLTS.ABA.Composition.abaNetwork_inputEnabled} and \leandeclnamed{composedComponents_compatible}{PLTS.ABA.Composition.composedComponents_compatible}.
  A corruption replaces the program of the process it names (D23), here as at the protocol: the round loop carries the flag, its own half of the $\mathrm{fail}$ transition writes it, every other transition is enabled only while it is not set, and in place of those transitions the replaced program is the self-loop \leandeclnamed{corruptedIdle}{PLTS.ABA.Composition.RoundLoopStep.corruptedIdle} on every non-silent label outside \leandecl{PLTS.ABA.Composition.roundLoopActsAt} --- its graded-agreement call, its coin call and its DECIDED send --- so it takes the round's return as an input.
  The ABA network holds the other half: its $\mathrm{fail}$ transition carries the guard $k \notin F \wedge |F| < f$, and it takes the ABA return, the replaced round loop's at either bit included, on one unguarded transition.
  The graded-agreement coordinate of a composed state is the state of an instance family: the recorded corruptions and a finite map holding the instance of every round the family has touched, each keeping its round variables and its network state from its first owned transition on, and a round outside the map reading as the initial instance under the recorded corruptions.
  The processes hold those round variables too (D22).
  What no process holds is the network state of a round, and a round instance, carrying no termination flag, takes a send or a delivery at a process the protocol has terminated.
  The graded-agreement family occupies the left argument of the outer parallel composition, which is the refinable position.
  Full construction: \leandecl{PLTS.ABA.ABDY.composed}, over \leandecl{PLTS.ABA.Composition.roundLoopProgram} and \leandecl{PLTS.ABA.Composition.ABANetwork}.
\end{definition}
