\begin{definition}[The gather-based sides and their protocol-shaped systems]
  \label{def:aba-gather-side}
  \lean{PLTS.ABA.GSub.liftedLowG}\lean{PLTS.ABA.GSub.liftedIdealG}\lean{PLTS.ABA.GSub.liftedPairG}\lean{PLTS.ABA.GSub.gActLow}\lean{PLTS.ABA.GSub.gActIdeal}\lean{PLTS.ABA.GSub.gActPair}\lean{PLTS.ABA.lowSideG}\lean{PLTS.ABA.idealSideG}\lean{PLTS.ABA.pairSideG}\lean{PLTS.ABA.composedG}\lean{PLTS.ABA.hybridG1}\lean{PLTS.ABA.hybridG2}\leanok
  \uses{def:aba-gbca-gather-low, def:aba-gbcasub, def:aba-composed, def:idle-family, def:process-algebra, def:relabel}
  Each reading of the two-gather round is read over the extended alphabet $\mathrm{NLab}(n)$ along the label pullback $\mathrm{gPull}$ of Definition~\ref{def:aba-gbcasub} --- the same read-back that lifts the round specification --- so a round-tagged label, a Byzantine drive included, takes the reading's own row and every other extended label idles.
  Each lifted reading is gathered into the $\mathbb{N}$-indexed family over the ownership map $\mathrm{gOwns}$, with $\mathrm{fail}(k)$ the broadcast that corrupts every round's coordinates in lockstep (D1): the sides $\mathrm{lowSideG}$, $\mathrm{idealSideG}$ and $\mathrm{pairSideG}$.
  Each side, beside the round loops, the ABA-side network and the coin oracle and through the two hiding frames of the composed reading's pipeline, gives a protocol-shaped system: $\mathrm{composedG}(P)$, $\mathrm{hybridG1}(P)$ and $\mathrm{hybridG2}(P)$.
  The first reads the gather-based protocol as a composition, and stands to the gather-based implementation exactly as $\mathrm{composed}(P)$ of Definition~\ref{def:aba-composed} stands to the direct one; the other two are the stages the substitution below passes through.
  Full construction: \leandecl{PLTS.ABA.composedG}.
\end{definition}
