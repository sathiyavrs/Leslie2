\begin{definition}[Idle padding and instance families]
  \label{def:idle-family}
  \lean{PLTS.System.withIdle}\lean{PLTS.InstanceFamilyState}\lean{PLTS.InstanceFamilyState.applyBroadcast}\lean{PLTS.System.family}\leanok
  \uses{def:process-algebra}
  Idle self-loops on the labels a component does not own make the full synchronisation of Definition~\ref{def:process-algebra} emulate sync-set composition.
  The $\mathbb{N}$-indexed family of instances $inst(r)$ runs on the state \leandecl{PLTS.InstanceFamilyState}, which holds the broadcast labels applied so far and a finite map of the instances the family has touched.
  A round outside the map is untouched and reads as the initial instance under the recorded broadcasts, \leandecl{PLTS.InstanceFamilyState.untouchedInstance}, so a round first touched after a corruption starts from the initial instance with that corruption applied.
  The family has four kinds of transition.
  On $\tau$ the instance at one round of the map moves.
  On a label a round owns the instance at that round moves, in the map or not, and the round enters the map.
  On a broadcast label the broadcast is recorded and its action applied to every instance of the map, \leandecl{PLTS.InstanceFamilyState.applyBroadcast}, so the action reaches every round.
  On every other label the family idles.
  The silent transitions range over the rounds of the map, which are finitely many at every state.
  Full construction: \leandecl{PLTS.System.withIdle} and \leandecl{PLTS.System.family}.
  Families of LTS instances are LTS.\lean{PLTS.System.family_isLTS}\leanok
\end{definition}
