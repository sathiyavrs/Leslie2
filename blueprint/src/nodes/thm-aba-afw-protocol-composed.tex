\begin{theorem}[The gather-based composition]
  \label{thm:aba-afw-protocol-composed}
  \lean{PLTS.ABA.AFW.toPair}\lean{PLTS.ABA.AFW.ProtocolRel}\lean{PLTS.ABA.AFW.protocolSim}\lean{PLTS.ABA.AFW.protocol_composed}\leanok
  \uses{def:aba-afw-protocol, def:aba-afw-sides, thm:forward-sound, def:achievable}
  The gather-based protocol is forward-simulated by its composed reading, and hence every trace distribution it achieves is achieved there.
  The relation is a function rather than a correspondence: the round loops and the coin oracle are shared objects, the ABA-side network is the DECIDED pools beside the corrupted set, and each round's instance is the view $\mathrm{toPair}$ of the flat state.
  Assembling that view undoes the two rearrangements the flat reading performs --- the boxes are transposed back out of the round records the $n$ processes hold, and each fabric is sliced out of the adversary's single tagged pool family.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}\leanok
  \uses{def:aba-afw-protocol, def:aba-afw-sides, def:forwardsim}
  Every row writes the acting process's round record and pools at most one tagged message, and one lemma says what that does to the view: the round the row names reads as the one-point update of each of its coordinates.
  What a row then owes is pool algebra --- which of the six slices takes the message and which are left alone --- and slicing commutes with a pool write in exactly those two ways.
  On that basis each row of the implementation is answered by a row of the round instance: a send and a delivery are internal to the instance, so the composed reading answers them with one of its own silent rules, the adversary's authenticity conjunct becoming membership in the sliced pool; the graded-agreement call and return are the instance's own call and return.
  The remaining labels never reach the implementation.
  The ABA interface, the coin handshake, the DECIDED rows and the fused coin return move the round loop and the ABA-side network while the family of rounds stands still; corruption is one broadcast, the corrupted set the adversary holds being the corrupted set of every fabric under the same guard; and the two Byzantine graded-agreement drives have no row at the process they name.
  A program's row on a label of $\mathrm{stageOwn}(j)$ is a row of the implementation, which is what lets each of those cases rule the others out.
\end{proof}
