\begin{theorem}[The round's graded-agreement composition refines its specification]
  \label{thm:aba-gbca-composition-by-abdy-refines-specification}
  \lean{PLTS.ABA.GBCA.ByABDY.refinesSpecification}\lean{PLTS.ABA.GBCA.ByABDY.specificationRelation_transition}\lean{PLTS.ABA.GBCA.ByABDY.composition_projects}\lean{PLTS.ABA.GBCA.specificationLabelMap}\lean{PLTS.ABA.GBCA.specificationOverRoundAlphabet}\lean{PLTS.ABA.GBCA.ByABDY.specificationRelation_init}\lean{PLTS.ABA.GBCA.ByABDY.refinesSpecification_failAct}\lean{PLTS.ABA.GBCA.ByABDY.specificationCorruptionAct}\leanok
  \uses{def:aba-gbca-instance-by-abdy, def:aba-gbca-algorithm-by-abdy, def:aba-gbca-spec, def:forwardsim}
  For all parameters $P$ and every round $r$, the round-$r$ composition $\mathrm{composition}(P,r)$ is forward-simulated by the round-$r$ GBCA specification instance, read over the round's interface.
  The relation reads the specification's $call$, $ret$ and $F$ off the composed state and witnesses its $excluded$ and $grade$ by patterns of received messages: an \emph{exclusion witness} for every member of $excluded$, and an $n{-}f$ ECHO5 quorum for each half of the grade guard.
  Exclusions are fired on demand: a return met by a specification state whose $excluded$ lacks the bit that return needs excluded is matched by the two-step weak run $\mathrm{bindUnset};\mathrm{ret}$, and every return --- grade-0 returns included --- does the same case split on $excluded$, however many returns preceded it.
  The relation holds at the initial states and survives the corruption broadcast.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}\leanok
  \uses{def:aba-gbca-instance-by-abdy, def:aba-gbca-algorithm-by-abdy, def:aba-gbca-spec, def:forwardsim, def:weakstep}
  The route has two halves.
  The first half reads a transition of the composition as a transition of the algorithm of Definition~\ref{def:aba-gbca-algorithm-by-abdy}.
  The composed state is the pair of the $n$ processes' round variables and the round's network state, which are exactly the components the composition puts together, so every transition of the composition is the algorithm's transition at that same state, one step for one step.
  Its label is carried by the partial map $\mathrm{specificationLabelMap}$, which identifies the Byzantine call and return transitions and the call loop of round $r$ with the specification labels they stand for, and along which the specification instance is read back by the construction $\mathrm{mapIdle}$ of Definition~\ref{def:aba-protocol}.
  The second half matches one transition of the algorithm by a weak run of the specification.
  The relation is checked constructor by constructor, and the work is in the witnesses it carries for the two fields the protocol does not hold.
  A member $b$ of $excluded$ is witnessed either by a quorum of $n{-}f$ received $\mathrm{ECHO}\,(1-b)$ messages or by a set of $n{-}f$ processes each corrupted or committed by its write-once $\mathit{sentVote}$ to a payload other than $b$; each half of the grade guard is witnessed by an $n{-}f$ $\mathrm{ECHO5}$ quorum at its own payload.
  All of these are $F$-blind and monotone in the delivered sets, in the write-once send fields and in $F$, so they survive every step and every $\mathrm{fail}$.
  The clause $bound\_excluded$ fixes $excluded$ by an equation on the round's bound bit: empty while the bit is unwritten, and the singleton of the bit's complement once a return has written it.
  The bit the round holds therefore supplies the guard $(1-bnd) \in excluded$ of a return that announces it.
  Two quorum intersections carry the argument: either exclusion witness makes an $n{-}f$ $\mathrm{VOTE}\,b$ quorum impossible forever, that quorum being the only source of a grade-$\ge 1$ witness for $b$; and an $n{-}f$ $\mathrm{ECHO5}\,v$ quorum cannot coexist with an $n{-}f$ $\mathrm{ECHO5}\,\bot$ quorum, which is grade-2 / grade-0 exclusivity.
  A return of $v$ met by a state with $1-v \notin excluded$ derives from its own guard a correct $\mathrm{BIND}\,v$ sender, whose $n{-}f$ $\mathrm{VOTE}\,v$ quorum both witnesses $1-v$ excluded and refutes any witness for $v$, and refines to an $\mathrm{INPUT}\,v$ quorum supplying the quorum guard and the $f{+}1$ $\mathrm{InputSupport}$ count of the exclusion (D8, D15); a grade-0 return names no bit, so its witness names some bit instead, by a split on whether any correct process ever voted a bit.
  Every such count passes through the invariant clause $input\_support$, that a correct multicast of $\langle\mathrm{INPUT},b\rangle$ is by a genuine holder of $b$ or already witnesses $f{+}1$ distinct processes each a genuine holder of $b$ or corrupted, and reading one at the specification is a rewrite along $call = \mathit{input}$ and the aligned corrupted sets.
  A corruption is matched by the specification's own $\mathrm{fail}$, and every delivery, relay and protocol-send transition by a specification stutter.
  The two halves compose: a step of the composition on $l$ is a step of the algorithm on $\mathrm{specificationLabelMap}(l)$, whose matching run is a weak transition read segment by segment along the section of $\mathrm{specificationLabelMap}$ that returns $l$; the specification is input-enabled on the Byzantine call and the Byzantine return, so that matching run needs no transition of its own (D11).
  The initial states are related, and the corruption broadcast preserves the relation, the network state's corrupted set being the one the algorithm reads and the two budget guards the same proposition (D1).
\end{proof}
