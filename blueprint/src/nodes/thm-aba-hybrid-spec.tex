\begin{theorem}[The hybrid refines the ABA specification]
  \label{thm:aba-hybrid-spec}
  \lean{PLTS.ABA.HybridState}\lean{PLTS.ABA.Invariant}\lean{PLTS.ABA.AbstractState}\lean{PLTS.ABA.hybridSpecificationStateRelation}\lean{PLTS.ABA.hybridSpecificationRelation}\lean{PLTS.ABA.hybridRefinesSpecification}\lean{PLTS.ABA.hybrid_spec}\leanok
  \uses{def:aba-hybrid, def:aba-spec, def:aba-core, def:aba-gbca-spec, def:aba-wcc-spec, def:aba-labels, def:process-algebra, def:idle-family, def:forwardsim, def:weakstep, def:pmfrel, def:achievable, thm:forward-sound}
  For all parameters $P$,
  \[
    \operatorname{ATD}(\mathrm{hybrid}(P)) \;\subseteq\;
    \operatorname{ATD}(\mathrm{spec}(P)).
  \]
  The two systems are compared on the ABA interface alone, namely $\mathrm{callABA}(i,b)$, $\mathrm{retABA}(i,b)$, $\mathrm{fail}(i)$ and $\tau$; the sub-protocol API, the labels $\mathrm{callG}(r,i,b)$, $\mathrm{retG}(r,i,g,d)$, $\mathrm{callW}(r,i)$ and $\mathrm{retW}(r,i,c)$ for $r \in \mathbb{N}$, $i \in [n]$, bits $b$, $c$ and $d$ and graded outcomes $g \in \{(0,2), (1,2), (0,1), (1,1), (\bot,0)\}$, is hidden at the outer frame of $\mathrm{hybrid}(P)$. Headline: \leandecl{PLTS.ABA.hybrid_spec}.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}\leanok
  \uses{def:aba-hybrid, def:aba-spec, def:aba-core, def:aba-gbca-spec, def:aba-wcc-spec, def:aba-labels, def:process-algebra, def:idle-family, def:forwardsim, def:weakstep, def:pmfrel, def:achievable, thm:forward-sound}
  One probabilistic forward simulation carries the hybrid to the ABA specification, \leandecl{PLTS.ABA.hybridRefinesSpecification}, and it is built by hand and checked row by row.
  A concrete state is the quadruple $\mathrm{HybridState}(P)$ of the round specifications, the $n$ round loops, the ABA network and the coin oracle, which is the state of $\mathrm{hybrid}(P)$ itself.
  The relation is \leandecl{PLTS.ABA.hybridSpecificationRelation}, the Dirac lift of \leandecl{PLTS.ABA.hybridSpecificationStateRelation}, and $\mathrm{hybridSpecificationStateRelation}$ is the conjunction of an invariant $\mathrm{Invariant}$ on the concrete state with an abstraction predicate $\mathrm{AbstractState}$ tying it to the abstract one.
  Both read the two ABA components through the accessors of Definition~\ref{def:aba-core}, so the whole relation is written in the coordinates the system already has and no change of system precedes it.
  Take a state of $\mathrm{hybrid}(P)$ satisfying $\mathrm{Invariant} \wedge \mathrm{AbstractState}$ and a row out of it.
  $\mathrm{AbstractState}$ pins the corrupted set, the returns and $mode = flipEnabled$, so the abstract state never fires \leandeclnamed{coinFlip}{PLTS.ABA.SpecStep.coinFlip}, and it is two-phase on the abstract state's $val$ (D16).
  Outside that disjunction it pins the ghost record as well: it agrees with the committed input at every correct process, in both phases.
  There are three cases.
  First, a hidden row: the abstract state answers by a stutter in the $\mathrm{AbstractState}$ frame, the coin's resolving call under the constant coupling that sends every outcome to the one unchanged abstract state.
  Second, the first $\mathrm{retABA}$ of the run: the abstract state answers by a single weak step that binds, fills, decides and returns, its bind licensed by the $f{+}1$ input support carried in the invariant and its grade-2 certificate obtained by a pigeonhole on the $n-f$ DECIDED tally behind the return.
  Third, a $\mathrm{fail}$ row: the abstract state answers by the corruption broadcast.
  Either way the relation is restored, so $\mathrm{hybridSpecificationRelation}$ is a probabilistic forward simulation, and soundness (Theorem~\ref{thm:forward-sound}) turns it into the stated inclusion, \leandecl{PLTS.ABA.hybrid_spec}.
\end{proof}
