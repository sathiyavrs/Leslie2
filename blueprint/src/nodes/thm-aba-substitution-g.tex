\begin{theorem}[The gather-based substitution]
  \label{thm:aba-substitution-g}
  \lean{PLTS.ABA.RlowAll}\lean{PLTS.ABA.RidealAll}\lean{PLTS.ABA.RpairAll}\lean{PLTS.ABA.famLowSimProbG}\lean{PLTS.ABA.famIdealSimProbG}\lean{PLTS.ABA.famPairSimProbG}\lean{PLTS.ABA.substSimG}\lean{PLTS.ABA.substitutionG}\leanok
  \uses{def:aba-gather-side, def:aba-hybrid, thm:gbca-tier-refines, thm:gbca-pair-refines, thm:mapidle-cong, thm:gbca-family-refines, thm:precong-parallel, thm:precong-abstract, thm:precong-relabel, thm:forward-sound}
  Three stages carry the gather-based composed reading to the protocol-shaped specification:
  \[
    \mathrm{composedG}(P) \;\sqsubseteq\; \mathrm{hybridG1}(P) \;\sqsubseteq\; \mathrm{hybridG2}(P) \;\sqsubseteq\; \mathrm{hybrid}(P).
  \]
  Each stage is one substitution inside the round --- the broadcast substitution, the gather substitution, the counting refinement --- lifted over the extended alphabet by the read-back congruence, lifted to the $\mathbb{N}$-indexed family by the family congruence under the lockstep broadcast compatibility, and run under the composed reading's own context through the four congruences of the pipeline.
  The third stage lands on $\mathrm{hybrid}(P)$ itself, the system the core simulation starts from, so the two implementation chains share every link from there up.
  Chaining the stages gives the simulation $\mathrm{substSimG}$, and chaining their soundness inclusions gives $\operatorname{ATD}(\mathrm{composedG}(P)) \subseteq \operatorname{ATD}(\mathrm{hybrid}(P))$.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}\leanok
  \uses{def:aba-gather-side, def:aba-hybrid, thm:gbca-tier-refines, thm:gbca-pair-refines, thm:mapidle-cong, thm:gbca-family-refines, thm:precong-parallel, thm:precong-abstract, thm:precong-relabel, thm:forward-sound}
  Theorem~\ref{thm:mapidle-cong} lifts each per-round simulation along $\mathrm{gPull}$; the family congruence of Theorem~\ref{thm:gbca-family-refines} lifts the result to the sides, its broadcast hypothesis discharged by the lockstep-corruption compatibility of each relation; and each family substitution runs under the syntactically identical context by $\mathrm{parallel\_right}$ followed by $\mathrm{abstract}$, $\mathrm{relabel}$ and $\mathrm{abstract}$, exactly as the substitution of Theorem~\ref{thm:aba-substitution} does.
  The stages compose by Result~2, and the inclusion is the composite of the three stage inclusions.
\end{proof}
