\begin{theorem}[Gather broadcast substitution]
  \label{thm:gather-broadcast-substitution}
  \lean{PLTS.ABA.Gather.BroadcastSubstitutionRelation}\lean{PLTS.ABA.Gather.broadcastSubstitution}\leanok
  \uses{def:aba-gather-over-bracha, def:aba-gather-over-broadcast-specification, thm:brb-refines, thm:forwardsim-congruence}
  The gather implementation over Bracha instances is forward-simulated by the gather implementation over broadcast specifications.
  The relation holds the gather tier --- the programs with their stores and the gather network with its core --- equal, beside the broadcast refinement relation of Theorem~\ref{thm:brb-refines} at each of the $2n$ coordinates.
  The proof is an application of Theorem~\ref{thm:forwardsim-congruence} and nothing else.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}\leanok
  \uses{def:aba-gather-over-bracha, def:aba-gather-over-broadcast-specification, thm:brb-refines, def:forwardsim}
  The broadcast refinement of Theorem~\ref{thm:brb-refines} is read along the pullback naming each coordinate by the congruence for the partial pullback, lifted over each of the two families by the congruence for the synchronised product, joined across the two families by transitivity with one family held in each system, carried through the parallel composition with the gather tier held, then through the hiding of the instance's events and the restriction to its interface.
  The composite relation the congruences produce has an existential middle state; it is rewritten to the stated one, the middle state being determined by the equalities.
  Nothing about the rows is argued: a broadcast return, which writes a program's record, is a step of the instance that Theorem~\ref{thm:brb-refines} answers, and the gather tier is held equal throughout.
\end{proof}
