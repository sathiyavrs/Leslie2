\begin{theorem}[Family congruence and the graded-agreement family refinement]
  \label{thm:gbca-family-refines}
  \lean{PLTS.ForwardSimulation.family}\lean{PLTS.ABA.gbcaSpecificationFamily}\lean{PLTS.ABA.substitutionRelationFamily}\lean{PLTS.ABA.GBCA.ByABDY.refinesSpecification_untouchedFirstStep}\lean{PLTS.ABA.familySubstitution}\lean{PLTS.ABA.familySubstitutionSimulation}\leanok
  \uses{thm:aba-gbca-composition-by-abdy-refines-specification, def:aba-gbca-instance-by-abdy, def:idle-family, lem:hyperstep-weakstep}
  Per-instance forward simulations lift to the $\mathbb{N}$-indexed families of Definition~\ref{def:idle-family}, along the relation asking for the same recorded broadcasts, the same rounds in the two maps and related instances at every round.
  The lift asks two things beside the per-instance simulations: the broadcast actions preserve the per-instance relations, and from a related pair of untouched instances a concrete step on a label the round owns is matched by an abstract transition on that label followed by a silent run.
  Applied to Theorem~\ref{thm:aba-gbca-composition-by-abdy-refines-specification}, the lift says that the family $\mathrm{gbcaInstanceFamily}(P)$ of round instances is forward-simulated, round by round, by the family $\mathrm{gbcaSpecificationFamily}(P)$ of round specifications read over the same interface.
  Both families are LTS, so the LTS correspondence turns this into a probabilistic forward simulation, applied once, at family level.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}\leanok
  \uses{thm:aba-gbca-composition-by-abdy-refines-specification, def:idle-family, lem:hyperstep-weakstep}
  The two families are built by the same construction from the same ownership map $\mathrm{roundOwnsLabel}$.
  A silent step at a round of the map, and an owned step at such a round, moves the one instance that owns it and is matched instance-wise by Theorem~\ref{thm:aba-gbca-composition-by-abdy-refines-specification}, the matching weak run embedded in the family at that round.
  An owned step at an untouched round is matched by the second hypothesis: the family takes no silent transition at an untouched round, so the matching run begins with the transition on the owned label, and \leandecl{PLTS.ABA.GBCA.ByABDY.refinesSpecification_untouchedFirstStep} supplies it, the first transition of a round being a call matched by the specification's call.
  A corruption broadcast is matched by the abstract family's own broadcast, using the compatibility lemma of Theorem~\ref{thm:aba-gbca-composition-by-abdy-refines-specification}, and a label owned by no round leaves both families in place.
  Each round instance is an LTS and so is each specification instance, so a family of them is an LTS by Definition~\ref{def:idle-family}, and the correspondence (Lemma~\ref{lem:hyperstep-weakstep}) applies once, at family level, yielding a probabilistic forward simulation along the Dirac lift of the relation.
\end{proof}
