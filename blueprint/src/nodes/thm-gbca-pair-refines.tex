\begin{theorem}[Two-gather round refinement]
  \label{thm:gbca-pair-refines}
  \lean{PLTS.ABA.GBCA.cnt_heavy_of_subMap}\lean{PLTS.ABA.GBCA.members_agree}\lean{PLTS.ABA.GBCA.members_heavy_light}\lean{PLTS.ABA.GBCA.PairInv}\lean{PLTS.ABA.GBCA.DeadEv}\lean{PLTS.ABA.GBCA.PairRel}\lean{PLTS.ABA.GBCA.pairRefines}\leanok
  \uses{def:aba-gbca-pair, def:aba-gbca-spec}
  The two-gather round is forward-simulated by the GBCA specification instance (Transition System~2).
  The relation reads the specification's $call$, $ret$ and $F$ off the pair and certifies its $dead$ and $grade$ on the gather core families: a bit $b$ is excluded under
  \[
    \mathrm{DeadEv}(b) \;=\; \exists\, \mathit{Cs},\ cores_1 = \mathit{Cs} \,\wedge\, \forall\, U \in \mathit{Cs},\ \#\{(k, b) \in U\} < |U| - f,
  \]
  the $A$-latch by a member of the second family heavy at one bit, and the $C$-latch by a member of the second family light everywhere; the families being write-once, every certificate is frozen when written.
  Kills fire on demand, as the weak burst $\mathrm{bindUnset}; \mathrm{ret}$ under the first return that needs the exclusion.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}\leanok
  \uses{def:aba-gbca-pair, def:aba-gbca-spec, def:forwardsim}
  Three member-counting facts carry the returns, each an arithmetic consequence of the pairwise $n - f$ intersection bound at $3f < n$.
  A returned map heavy at $x$ --- all but $f$ entries --- makes every family member heavy at $x$, the member sitting below the map up to $f$ entries (the member form of Lemma~13 of~\cite{AFW25}); two members heavy at $x$ and $y$ force $x = y$ on the $\ge n - f \ge 2f + 1$ shared entries (Proposition~14); and a member heavy at $x$ leaves every other value at most $f$ entries on any member (Lemma~18, and the $A$/$C$ exclusivity of the grade latch).
  A candidate $v$ arriving at the second gather is heavy in the first family, so a standing $\mathrm{DeadEv}(v)$ is refuted and the specification's honest call row is enabled; conversely an $A$- or $B$-return of $v$ certifies $\mathrm{DeadEv}(1 - v)$ from its own quorum, and the kill's $f{+}1$ support and the $B$-return's dissent count are read off the more than $f$ entries of the returned map that do not carry $v$, each an honest caller or corrupted.
  A $C$-return names no bit; its everywhere-light member certifies some exclusion by a case split on whether any member is heavy at a bit, and either killed bit closes the return since a certified-dead bit carries no return evidence in any extension.
  The invariant ties each gather's committed entries to honest calls and carries the provenance of second-gather calls back to first-gather returns; every internal row preserves it, and corruption is answered by the specification's own $\mathrm{fail}$ under the lockstep of the two corrupted sets.
\end{proof}
